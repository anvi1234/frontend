Shipping Policy

Ashirvad Rudraksh and Gems

1. Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days after successful payment confirmation. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays.

During high order volume periods, shipment processing may take slightly longer. Customers will be notified via email or phone in case of significant delays.

2. Delivery Timeline

  • Within India: Estimated delivery time is 3–7 working days.
  • Remote or heavy shipment orders may require additional delivery time.
  • International shipping (if applicable): Delivery may take approximately 10–20 working days depending on destination country.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated automatically at checkout based on:

  • Delivery location
  • Order weight
  • Shipping method selected

Delivery delays may occasionally occur due to courier or external factors beyond our control.

4. Shipment Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping details. Address changes should be requested immediately after placing the order. We are not responsible for delivery issues caused by incorrect addresses.

5. Shipment Confirmation & Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email containing tracking details. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become active.

6. Customs Duties & Taxes

International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes, or import fees imposed by destination country authorities. These charges are the responsibility of the customer.

7. Damaged or Lost Shipments

  • Please inspect your package before accepting delivery.
  • If the package appears damaged, refuse delivery or record proof immediately.
  • Contact the courier company to file a claim if damage occurs during shipping.
  • Keep all packaging materials for claim processing.

Ashirvad Rudraksh and Gems is not responsible for courier damages but will assist customers in the resolution process.
